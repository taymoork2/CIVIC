## Verified directions

**Tide.** Medium: 80% of cap bar 1. Large: 80% of bar 3. HE full: bar 5. Deep-fill guidance calls for more but gives no quantity. Follow garment care, sort and check colorfastness; detergent precedes clothes. No cap-to-volume calibration was established. Ingredient detail cards identify 29 ingredients, including four enzymes; fragrance is disclosed. [Exact SmartLabel](https://smartlabel.pg.com/en-us/00030772121108.html), updated September 8, 2026.

**Downy Free & Gentle.** The exact record starts normal loads at half the softener dispenser, approximately ¼ US cup, with more for tough odors. Add usual detergent first; deliver the rinse aid through the softener compartment or during rinse. Keep the approximation and dispenser context. The six disclosed ingredients and CAS/function metadata are in JSON. The exact safety panel prohibits bleach; family guidance also excludes liquid fabric softener. [Exact SmartLabel](https://smartlabel.pg.com/en-us/00030772103111.html), updated September 5, 2024; [family directions](https://downy.com/en-us/fabric-softener-tips/how-to-use-downy/how-to-use-rinse-and-refresh).

**Lysol line 1833.** HE/front-load rinse: two capfuls, each to line 2. Standard-machine rinse: two capfuls, each to line 1. Use the softener compartment or add directly to rinse; maintain 16 minutes of contact. Main-wash mixing with detergent is excluded. Presoak: two line-1 capfuls per US gallon of cold water, fully submerged for 15 minutes, followed by thorough rinsing or machine washing as directed. Not for wool, silk or materials that cannot be machine washed. The concentrate carries corrosive/eye-damage warnings and protective-eyewear instructions. [Selected SmartLabel](https://rbnainfo.com/smart-label.php?productLineId=1833), updated September 18, 2024.

**OxiClean Free.** For regular wash loads, scoop line 1; heavily soiled loads use more without a precise increment. Add powder to the drum before clothing; do not place it in dispensers. Presoak: scoop lines 2–4 per US gallon, dissolve first, soak for the stated 1–6-hour interval, then wash. Do not convert scoop marks to grams. Respect garment temperatures, test colorfastness, and exclude wool/wool blends, silk/silk blends, leather and dry-clean-only fabrics. [Booster directions](https://www.oxiclean.com/en/stain-solutions/what-are-laundry-boosters-and-when-to-use-them), [Free product directions](https://www.oxiclean.com/en/Products/Stain%20Fighters/OxiClean%20Versatile%20Stain%20Remover%20Free), [disclosure](https://churchdwight.com/ingredient-disclosure/laundry-fabric-care/40002383-oxi-versatile-stain-remover-fragrance-free.aspx).

**White Revive Paks.** One intact Pak for regular/lightly soiled loads; the manufacturer's booster guide specifies two for large/heavily soiled loads, including HE heavy-soil use. Place in the drum before laundry, with detergent following its usual delivery route. Do not open the Pak. Presoak directions require full dissolution and immediate use, then washing; the retrieved primary pages do not establish a Pak-to-water ratio. The product page states 1–6 hours and warns against storing the solution. Cold-water guidance calls for prior dissolution in hot water; garment temperature limits remain controlling. Colorfast washable laundry only, with the same wool/silk/leather/dry-clean-only exclusions. The disclosed formula includes oxygen bleach, protease, amylase, brightener and fragrance. [Product directions](https://www.oxiclean.com/en/products/stain-fighters/white-revive-paks-chlorine-bleach-alternative), [booster directions](https://www.oxiclean.com/en/stain-solutions/what-are-laundry-boosters-and-when-to-use-them), [disclosure](https://churchdwight.com/ingredient-disclosure/laundry-fabric-care/40500413-oxi-white-revive-power-pak-laundry-stain-rmvr.aspx).

**Downy Ball.** Manufacturer guidance is for non-HE machines and fabric conditioner: measure the compatible product, push the top down to fill, pull the ring to seal, then place the ball in the washer for automatic rinse delivery. No retrieved manufacturer instruction specifically permits this owner's rinse aid or sanitizer in the ball. [Licensed manufacturer](https://www.nehemiahmfg.com/?portfolio=downy-ball).

**Wool dryer balls.** General appliance-maker guidance places them with dryer-safe laundry and stores them dry afterward. Its three-ball example, lifespan and recharge instructions are general context, not verified directions for the owner's unbranded pack. Do not assign a high-heat setting, exact ball count or guaranteed drying-time reduction. [Whirlpool general guidance](https://qr.whirlpool.com/blog/washers-and-dryers/how-to-use-wool-dryer-balls.html?fmt=png-alpha).

## Compatibility and evidence limits

- Downy's exact prohibition says bleach without limiting it to chlorine bleach. Both owner Oxi formulas disclose oxygen bleach. Treat this as a recorded bleach restriction; separate-cycle compatibility has not been established.
- Lysol and Downy both target rinse delivery. The selected sources do not authorize mixing them or sharing one dispenser dose. The later EPA master label includes optional rinse-additive exclusion wording; retain it as context.
- Both Oxi products are directed for use with ordinary laundry detergent under their route and fabric conditions. That does not establish permission to combine the two Oxi boosters.
- Oxi solutions must not be mixed/stored in sealed containers. Free FAQ guidance attributes pressure buildup to released oxygen. White Revive's product FAQ directs immediate use without storage.
- Source capabilities and benefit claims do not prove garment compatibility or justify automatically using an owned product.

The [EPA master label](https://www3.epa.gov/pesticides/chem_search/ppls/000777-00128-20231201.pdf) for registration 777-128 supplies additional concentration context, but it was approved after the selected Lysol source was discontinued. Its alternative cap sizes and placeholder doses do not calibrate the owner's cap. The selected SmartLabel's 16-minute rinse instruction is preserved despite varying times in its organism tables.

The Downy disclosure records an invalid CAS checksum for propylene glycol (57-55-7); White Revive records 57-55-6, which passes. White Revive repeats 2-Methoxynaphthalene. JSON preserves these source issues. The Free FAQ contains apparent template carryovers about fragrance/blue crystals; the exact Free ingredient disclosure controls formula identity. Downy's title includes “fabric softener,” while its operational explanation identifies a rinse that replaces softener.
